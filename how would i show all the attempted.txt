To capture and filter SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) traffic in Wireshark, follow these steps:

---

### **Step-by-Step Instructions**

#### **Method 1: Using Protocol Dropdown (If Available)**
1. **Start Capturing Traffic**:
   Open Wireshark, go to **View > Capture Options**, and select the appropriate network interface to capture traffic on.

2. **Apply Protocol-Specific Filter**:
   - Click the **Filter** icon (🔍) in the top-right corner or go to **View > Filter**.
   - In the filter window, under the **Protocol** dropdown, select **SMTP**.
   - Click **Apply**. This filters the display to show only packets Wireshark identified as SMTP based on port and handshake detection.

#### **Method 2: Port-Based Filter (if SMTP isn't listed in Protocol Dropdown)**
1. **Open Filter Window**:
   Go to **View > Filter** or click the filter icon (🔍).
2. **Enter Port-Based Filter**:
   In the filter field, type:
   ```
   tcp port 25 or tcp port 465 or tcp port 587
   ```
   (Replace `or` with `and` if you need to combine with other criteria, e.g., `tcp port 25 and ip.src == "192.168.1.100"` for a specific IP).
3. Click **Apply** to view filtered TCP packets on these ports.

---

### **Additional Refinements (Optional)**
